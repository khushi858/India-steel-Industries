How Orders Move

Enquiry To Delivery

A straight line from your specification sheet to material on your site.

01
Day 0
Enquiry

You share drawings, grade, quantity and the delivery site over call, email or WhatsApp. Our desk logs it into the order pipeline the same minute it lands, no waiting on forms.

02
Within 24h
Quotation

Our estimator prices against live yard stock and current mill rates. You get a costed, itemised offer within 24 hours, with grade certificates flagged upfront.

03
Day 1–2
Sourcing

Confirmed stock is pulled straight from warehouse racking; any shortfall is placed on order with our rolling mills, tracked against your PO number end to end.

04
Day 2–4
Fabrication

Material moves to the shop floor for cutting, bending, threading or galvanizing to your exact spec sheet, all done in-house so nothing waits on a third-party job worker.

05
Day 4
Quality Check

Every finished batch is matched heat number to heat number against its material test certificate before it clears the floor, so paperwork and product always agree.

06
Day 5+
Dispatch

Orders are crated or export-packed, tagged with MTC copies and handed to our pan-India logistics network for delivery straight to your site.

Tracked At Every Stage From the moment your enquiry lands to the crate leaving our yard, every stage is logged against your PO number, so you always know exactly where your order stands.
